About me

I’m Aldrich Chen (陈思蕲). I work in early-stage investing at Hillhouse, focusing on consumer AI software and hardware (AI2C) and AI infrastructure. Before that, I was at HongShan. I studied quantitative finance, electrical engineering, and physics at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, and co-founded AccSeed+ with friends.

I get interested in all sorts of things. When something catches my attention, I like to find the people working on it and have a conversation. We talk about business, and about how we each got here. Sometimes I build things myself. I’ve written tools for tracking new products, editing transcripts, and giving AI a memory. Some were for work; others were just to see if I could make them. I don’t always need to know whether something will be useful before I start. Feel free to say hello. I’m just as happy to talk about things outside work.
